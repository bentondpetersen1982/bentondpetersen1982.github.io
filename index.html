The problem

AI storytellers are convincing, but they can’t be trusted with the facts

Say “I find a bag of gold” and most AI stories hand you the gold. Names, prices and what you paid drift or vanish a few turns later. A promise in the dialogue is just words: nothing makes it happen. Without a world that remembers, a story can’t last more than a session.

The solution

The AI proposes.
The engine decides.

PSE keeps the world’s truth in its own records: who is where, who owns what, what was paid, what was promised. The AI reads the player, voices the characters and tells the story, but only what the engine has made true becomes part of the world.

Where PSE is

What works today, what’s being built, and where it’s going

Works today

  • The engine holds the world’s truth: people, places, things, money, time
  • Prices, payments, change and deals, counted exactly
  • Promises that are kept, by the person who made them or someone they send
  • Everyone you meet has a name, beliefs, goals and wants of their own
  • Claims the world can’t back up are never made true
  • Recorded live: one world, three runs, three different stories

Being built now

  • Long play: stories that run for days and weeks
  • Characters who remember you and change through what happens
  • Creating your own character, with traits and skills that grow through play
  • Keeping the cost of each turn flat as a story grows
  • The platform online, with accounts

Where it’s going

  • Author your own worlds and share them
  • Worlds from published books, sold by their authors
  • Share the stories you played in public forums
  • Voice and generated images
  • Access for developers

Worlds endure. Your stories do too.
