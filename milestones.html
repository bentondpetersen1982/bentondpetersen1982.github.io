Milestones

Where PSE is, and where it’s going

Each step below is a working part of the engine or the platform, not a promise on a calendar. Finished steps are marked as they land, and each one gets a post on the Updates page.

The road to the alpha and beta

Milestones

  1. Done · Sep–Nov 2026

    The engine and its rules

    The engine holds the world’s truth: people, places, things, money, time, deals and promises. The AI tells the story but cannot change what’s true. Started 9 September, about 1,300 tests.

  2. Done · 5 Nov 2026

    The demo reel

    One world, the same words typed three times, three different stories, with every coin and promise kept. Watch it.

  3. Done · 6 Nov 2026

    Alpha and beta sign-up open

    Anyone can put their name down to play first. Sign up.

  4. Up next

    Long play: stories with no length limit

    Characters who remember you and change through what happens, over days and weeks of play, while the cost of each turn stays flat however long the story runs.

  5. Then

    Your character, your skills

    Create your own character, with traits and skills that grow through play. Outcomes judged by skill and pressure rather than dice, where tactics like surprise or bad footing matter in any setting.

  6. Then

    The platform online

    Play in the browser with an account, no install. An outside security review before anyone outside the team plays.

  7. Then

    Closed alpha

    A few hundred players from the sign-up list. Earlier and rougher; your play and notes decide what gets fixed next.

  8. Then

    Larger beta

    A few thousand players, more polished, and proof the platform holds up when lots of people play at once.

  9. Later

    Launch, and worlds of your own

    Public launch. Author your own worlds and share them, worlds from published books sold by their authors, then voice and generated images.
