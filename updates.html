15–16 November 2026

Your character, worlds from nothing, and a goddess who summons you

Status: partly tested. The world and character screens have been clicked through in a browser, and the first short live session in a brand-new world has been played: it found two problems, both now fixed (below). Everything else is checked by the engine's own tests but not yet played live.

The character builder screen. After the world comes your character: name, look (age, gender, height, build, hair, eyes, skin, marks), an optional background (or let the storyteller write one), attributes, and skills typed in by name, with a list of suggestions across every genre. Fighting skills can take a specialty that grows faster. Traits can be suggested from your background. A points bar stays in view so you always see what you have left.

A profession, and money that fits the world. Pick from dozens of professions (blacksmith, hunter, scribe, soldier and more). Each gives its skills, its training, its tools, and a month of its wages in that world as savings, all at no cost. Some build the body or mind, so a soldier starts tougher. You can buy extra money with points, worth a month's wages each.

People you know. Name people your character already knows, choose how they feel about you at the start, and build their character sheets yourself if you want to. Romance is never chosen at the start: it has to happen in the story.

A world built from nothing. New Game now offers Rivermoor as a ready-made world, or a brand-new one. A new world starts empty: you write what you want, and the storyteller makes your starting region and the town you begin in from what you wrote. Towns are laid out (square, inn, shrines, streets) the first time you are in them, so you can walk into town and go anywhere by name.

Peoples and gods with the storyteller's help. Describe a people and the storyteller suggests their lifespan, what sets them apart, and their natural gifts, which a character of that people gets for free. Write a god's domain and it suggests a name, or let it suggest a whole pantheon. Every suggestion is yours to take, refuse, or edit first.

Summoned from another world. A character from another world can bring skills and knowledge this world hasn't reached, a background from their old life (a park ranger knows rescue; a marine starts fit), and simple modern implants.

The goddess. If your story says a goddess summons you, it now opens in her own place outside the world, a place you cannot reach unless she brings you there. She is a character in her own right, with her own reasons for summoning you. Talk with her; she chooses gifts that give you potential rather than power, and a persuasive character might talk her into one more. She sees the whole world, and hears you if you speak to her from it, though no answer comes. Rarely, when you sleep or are near death, she may appear in a dream, or draw you back to her, only to guide you toward why you were brought, or toward something more pressing.

From the first live session: the town the storyteller described didn't exist in the engine, so you couldn't walk into it; and the goddess never appeared. Both are fixed and waiting for the next live run.

13 November 2026

A big build day: worlds, maps, dungeons and companions

Status: built and checked by the engine's own tests, not yet player-tested. None of this has been played in a live story yet. Live play comes once the character builder is finished, and we'll post what holds up and what needs fixing.

Build your own world. A new world builder comes first on New Game, with a section for each part of the world: governments, coins, technology, whether magic exists and how it works, peoples, gods, laws and customs, the calendar, and how your character arrives (born here, or brought from another world). Every section is optional except the content rating. Anything you leave blank, the storyteller fills in when the story first needs it, and from then on it is as fixed as anything you wrote yourself.

Your character, priced fairly. We built the rules underneath the character builder: skills, six attributes, and traits and flaws, all bought from one point budget the world sets. You can write your own trait from what you want it to do, and the engine works out its effect and its price. The character builder screen itself is still to come.

A map that is made as you go. Your starting region is made when the story begins, and each new region is made the first time you cross into it, built from what is already known next door. You can travel along roads, rivers, tracks and passes. Caves, mines, crypts and ruins are made the first time someone goes in, with hidden passages, caches and traps. Searching depends on your eyes and the light.

Danger that is fair. Climbs, leaps, flooded passages and traps are faced with skill and the right gear, never dice by default. Only a truly deadly hazard can kill you. If your character dies, the story tells you how (as graphically as the world's rating allows), and you can go back to an earlier point or start again.

Your own map. Your character remembers where they have been, and that memory fades with time. With paper and charcoal and the new cartography skill, they can draw a map that lasts and can be carried, lost or sold. A topography skill reads the land and shortens journeys off the roads.

Companions. People can join you by agreeing to in the story, and they keep their own will: they follow you, face the same dangers, and may leave if their own life calls them away. They take the night watch in turn, the sharpest eyes first, and a member who is short of sleep is passed over.

Still to come: the character builder screen, encounters on the road, travel off the beaten paths, and then live play of all of it.

6 November 2026

The alpha sign-up is open

You can now put your name down to play Persistent Story Engine first.

It will open in two stages. First comes a closed alpha of a few hundred players: earlier, rougher, and the place where your play and your notes decide what gets fixed and built next. After that comes a larger beta of a few thousand players, more polished, and where we make sure the platform holds up when lots of people play at once.

Signing up takes a minute: an email, what kinds of stories you’d play, and which stage you’d like to join. Authors can also tell us if they’d like to bring a world of their own. Everyone on the list hears first when each stage opens. Dates are planned, not promised, and we’ll post them here as they firm up.

5 November 2026

The demo reel is out: one world, three stories

The first demo reel is up on the home page. A traveller walks into a village inn, asks for a room and a hot meal, pays, and waits for what was promised. We played it three times, typing exactly the same words each time, and got three different stories.

What stays the same is the truth of the world. Every coin paid and every bit of change, every deal, every key handed over and every promise made is kept by the engine, not by the AI. The people change how they talk and who brings your supper up the stairs, but nobody can talk their way around what actually happened. When the traveller claims to find a bag of gold under a table, the story finds only dust.

It was recorded live on the free AI tier; the waits between turns were trimmed, nothing else. The engine behind it was started on 9 September and is held together by about 1,300 tests.
