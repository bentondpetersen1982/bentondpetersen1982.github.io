The alpha sign-up is open

You can now put your name down to play Persistent Story Engine first.

It will open in two stages. First comes a closed alpha of a few hundred players: earlier, rougher, and the place where your play and your notes decide what gets fixed and built next. After that comes a larger beta of a few thousand players, more polished, and where we make sure the platform holds up when lots of people play at once.

Signing up takes a minute: an email, what kinds of stories you’d play, and which stage you’d like to join. Authors can also tell us if they’d like to bring a world of their own. Everyone on the list hears first when each stage opens. Dates are planned, not promised, and we’ll post them here as they firm up.

5 November 2026

The demo reel is out: one world, three stories

The first demo reel is up on the home page. A traveller walks into a village inn, asks for a room and a hot meal, pays, and waits for what was promised. We played it three times, typing exactly the same words each time, and got three different stories.

What stays the same is the truth of the world. Every coin paid and every bit of change, every deal, every key handed over and every promise made is kept by the engine, not by the AI. The people change how they talk and who brings your supper up the stairs, but nobody can talk their way around what actually happened. When the traveller claims to find a bag of gold under a table, the story finds only dust.

It was recorded live on the free AI tier; the waits between turns were trimmed, nothing else. The engine behind it was started on 9 September and is held together by about 1,300 tests.
